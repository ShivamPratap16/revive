Fintechs lose users halfway through KYC. Revive brings them back.

A backend that notices when someone gets stuck in an app's onboarding (an Aadhaar OTP that never arrived, a PAN name mismatch), reaches them with an in-app nudge and an AI voice call in their language, uses Gemini to understand why they stopped, and acts on it.

Who it's for

The fintech (the customer)Lending, broking and insurance apps that paid to acquire users who then quit mid-KYC.
Their usersPeople who wanted the loan but got stuck and didn't know what to do next.
How it plugs inThe app's backend sends Revive one event per KYC step. Revive does the rest and reports back.

How it works

01 · DETECTNotices who's stuck

A background worker finds users idle too long on a step (15 min for an OTP).

02 · REACH OUTNudges, then calls

An in-app message first, then a voice call, inside legal calling hours, never to DND numbers.

03 · UNDERSTANDAI reads the call

Gemini extracts why they stopped and what they want, after personal data is masked.

04 · ACT & REPORTFollows through

"Call me at 6" books a 6pm call; "don't call me" adds them to DND. All of it shows on the dashboard.

    Built like production code

    The interesting parts aren't the features, but what happens when things go wrong. Each claim below has a test that fails when the safeguard is removed.

    Retries never double-countIdempotent events and webhooks (unique keys + ON CONFLICT).
    Concurrency-safeRow locks: without them, 18 of 20 simultaneous updates were lost.
    Never loses or repeats workTransactional outbox, leases and a reaper; idempotency keys so nobody's phone rings twice.
    Webhooks can't be fakedHMAC signatures, a 5-minute replay window, secret rotation.
    Personal data stays inAadhaar, PAN, phone numbers and names are masked before anything reaches the AI.
    AI output is verified, then measuredSchema-checked, quotes checked against the transcript. Gemini scored 6/6 on held-out calls vs 2/6 for keyword rules.
    Degrades instead of failingShared rate limiter, circuit breaker, retries with backoff, rule-based fallback.
